# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely full-time or in a hybrid arrangement, subject to manager approval.

## Conference and travel expense eligibility for remote employees
Fully remote employees ARE eligible to expense conference tickets and related travel under the standard [Expense Policy](./expense_policy.md), provided the conference is relevant to their role and approved in advance by their manager.

## Equipment
Remote employees receive a one-time $300 home-office stipend, expensed the same way as other approved purchases.
