# Expense Policy

Employees may expense costs directly related to their job duties, including:

- Conference tickets and professional development courses
- Travel (flights, trains, mileage) for approved business trips
- Software/tools required for work, up to $200/month without pre-approval

## Approval
- Expenses under $500 are auto-approved.
- Expenses over $500 require sign-off from your manager (see your [team member profile](../team/alice.md) for who that is).

## Eligibility note
Expense eligibility for conferences and travel also depends on your work arrangement. See the [Remote Work Policy](./remote_work_policy.md) for conditions that apply to remote employees specifically.
