# Leave Policy — PTO250

All full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, up to a maximum of 21 days per year.

## Requesting leave
- Submit requests through [Jira](../tools/jira.md) at least 5 business days in advance.
- Manager approval is required for any leave longer than 5 consecutive days.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the following calendar year. Anything beyond that is forfeited.
